App icon: colour

Round 3. 1. The mark (decided: E, pen and paper). 2. Its colour (open).

Which colour should the pen-and-paper icon be? The app keeps its violet accent in every option: the active nav item and the “New entry” button below are drawn in it, so each icon is seen beside it.

Moving the whole app’s accent to blue or green is a separate, larger change (violet appears about 400 times across the views).

V. Violet, as today

Option V

#755ff8 (violet-600). The icon is the app’s own accent, so it matches the active nav, the buttons and the badges. No other change.

B. Blue

Option B

#2563eb (blue-600). Calmer and more “notebook ink” than violet. The app itself stays violet, so the icon becomes a second brand colour beside it.

G. Green

Option G

#15803d (green-700). Warm and distinct in a row of tabs. The app stays violet, and green already means “succeeded” on the Jobs page, so the icon would borrow a status colour.
