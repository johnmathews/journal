App accent: violet or green

Should the app’s accent stay violet or turn green to match the new icon? Only the accent changes: layout, greys, type and the green “Succeeded” status badge stay as they are.

The accent is every primary button, the active nav item, unread badges, links, active filter chips, entity avatars, checkboxes and the focus outline: about 400 uses across 57 views, swapped by step (violet-500 → accent-500, and so on).

V. Violet, as today (green icon)

Option V

No restyle. The green icon sits beside a violet app. The primary button stays white on #8470ff, which is 3.6:1, below the 4.5:1 text needs (measured below); that would be fixed on its own.

F. Forest green, matching the icon

Option F

Accent-500 is the icon’s own #15803d, so icon and app are one colour. Dark enough for white button text (5.0:1). Close to the success green: “Succeeded” now reads like a link or a selected chip. In dark, the focus outline and checkboxes use accent-400 (#4cb874) to clear 3:1.

E. Emerald, a cooler green

Option E

Accent-500 #047857 leans toward teal, which sets it a little apart from the yellower success green. The icon would move to #047857 too. In dark, outlines and checkboxes use accent-400 (#34d399).
