Dashboard: when the range is too quiet

The dashboard opens on “Last 3 months”. Your last entry was on 16 Oct 2026, so that range holds 2 entries, below the 5 the charts need, and the home page says “Not enough data yet” while your account holds 77. With “All time” selected the charts draw as normal. What should the dashboard do when the selected range is this quiet?

The charts, the filter bar, the 5-entry threshold and the tiles stay as they are. No new colours. Counts are your real ones: last month 0, last 3 months 2, last 6 months 32, last year 77, all time 77.

A. Today

Option A

The empty card with the count and a hint to widen the range. You find the right range by trying the pills.

B. The empty card offers the ranges that have enough

Option B

The range stays on “Last 3 months”, and the card names the shortest range with 5 or more entries and “All time”, with their counts, as buttons. One click draws the charts. The dashboard never changes the range on its own.

C. Open on the shortest range that has enough

Option C

When the dashboard first loads and “Last 3 months” has fewer than 5 entries, it opens on the shortest range that has 5 or more (here “Last 6 months”, 32 entries) and says so in a line above the charts, with a link back. A range you pick yourself is never changed. The home page shows your writing on arrival, which is what it’s for, and the note keeps the switch visible.

D. Always draw the charts, with a note when they’re thin

Option D

The 5-entry gate goes. The charts draw whatever the count, and below 5 entries a line above them says how few there are, with a link to “All time”. Honest about the range you picked, but 13 weeks with one bar says little.
